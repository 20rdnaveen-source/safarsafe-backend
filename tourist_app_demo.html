Safety Tips

🌞 Girivalam safety

Carry water, wear comfortable footwear, and keep valuables secure while walking around Arunachala Hill.

🛕 Temple visit

Stay with your group in crowded areas and use official entrances and visible public routes.

🌙 Night safety

Prefer well-lit streets, avoid isolated areas, and share your live location with a trusted contact.

📱 Emergency readiness

Keep the Town Police Station, Women Police Station, hospital, and fire-service numbers saved.
